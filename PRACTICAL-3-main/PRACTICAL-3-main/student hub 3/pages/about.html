ABOUT STUDENTHUB

Connecting Students With Their Campus Life

StudentHub brings campus events, student resources, and support services together in one simple and accessible platform.

WHAT IS STUDENTHUB?

A Smarter Way to Stay Connected

StudentHub is a college student portal created to make campus life more organized, connected, and accessible.

From discovering upcoming events to finding student resources and support, StudentHub provides students with one convenient place to stay informed throughout their college journey.
